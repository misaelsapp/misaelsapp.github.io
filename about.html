About Me

I am an independent software developer and digital creator passionate about building innovative applications that combine technology, creativity, and practical problem-solving. My work spans desktop, mobile, web, and interactive experiences, with a strong focus on rapid prototyping and experimentation.

Over the years, I have worked with technologies such as Unity, .NET, Kotlin, Java, PHP, OpenCV, ML Kit, and various AI frameworks to develop applications ranging from games and productivity tools to computer vision and AI-powered solutions. I enjoy transforming ideas into functional products that are both technically solid and enjoyable to use.

Beyond software development, I also create digital artwork and 3D content using tools such as CorelDRAW and 3ds Max. This combination of technical and creative skills allows me to design complete experiences, from application logic and AI systems to visual assets and user interfaces.

What I Do

My work revolves around turning ideas into working products through rapid development, iterative testing, and creative problem-solving. Areas I frequently work on include:

  • Application Development: Building desktop, mobile, and web applications using modern technologies and development frameworks.
  • Game Development: Creating interactive experiences, gameplay systems, tools, and prototypes using Unity and related technologies.
  • Artificial Intelligence: Developing AI-powered features such as intelligent assistants, machine learning solutions, text-to-speech systems, and automation tools.
  • Computer Vision: Exploring image processing, object recognition, visual analysis, and camera-based applications using OpenCV and ML Kit.
  • Digital Art & 3D Content: Designing visual assets, illustrations, user interface elements, and 3D models that enhance software and interactive experiences.
  • Rapid Prototyping: Quickly transforming concepts into functional prototypes for testing, validation, and continuous improvement.

I enjoy experimenting with emerging technologies and combining multiple disciplines to create solutions that are innovative, practical, and visually engaging.

My Mission

My mission is to bridge technology and creativity by developing applications that solve real-world problems while delivering engaging user experiences. I believe that great software is not only functional, it should also be intuitive, visually appealing, and capable of creating meaningful value for its users.

Through continuous learning and experimentation, I strive to stay at the forefront of software development, artificial intelligence, and digital content creation. Every project is an opportunity to explore new ideas, challenge technical limitations, and transform concepts into reality.

Ultimately, my goal is to build products and experiences that inspire curiosity, improve productivity, and demonstrate how technology can be used creatively to make a positive impact.
